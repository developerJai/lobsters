# Comments

> A comprehensive guide to Comments.

---

## What is Comments?

Comments are the discussion threads under every story on the site. Logged-in members can post comments, reply to each other in nested threads, vote on what's valuable, and flag what breaks the rules — and the community's votes automatically decide which comments appear first. When someone replies to you or mentions you, you're notified in the site, and optionally by email or phone push.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Post comments and preview them before they go live
- Reply in nested threads up to 18 levels deep
- Edit your comment for up to 6 hours after posting
- Delete your comment for up to 14 days (and undelete it), or disown it after that
- Upvote comments (and take the vote back), or flag them with a reason within 7 days
- Wear one of your hats (role or affiliation badges) on a comment
- Receive reply and @mention notifications — always in your inbox, plus email or Pushover push if enabled
- Follow the newest comments site-wide (20 per page, with a "Last Read" marker and RSS feeds), review comments you've upvoted, and revisit your recent threads

---

## How It Works

1. **You post** — Write your comment under a story (previewing first if you like), optionally choosing a hat to wear. If you accidentally submit the same comment twice, the site quietly keeps just one.
2. **The site checks quality** — Comments that add nothing are automatically declined with a short message: "me too", "+1", "this", "tl;dr", thread bumps, comments that are only a quote, and emoji-only comments. Your comment also can't go through if the story has stopped accepting comments, if the comment you're answering was removed while you typed, or if you previously flagged the comment you're replying to.
3. **It appears in the thread** — Threads nest replies under the comments they answer. The best-received replies in each group rise to the top automatically, and the ordering is stable — comments don't shuffle around between page loads.
4. **The community responds** — Readers upvote or flag. Heavily downvoted comments (−5 and below) appear collapsed. Scores on comments less than 36 hours old stay hidden while they're near zero (between −2 and +4) to prevent bandwagon voting.
5. **People get notified** — The author of the comment you replied to, the story's author (if they follow their story), and anyone you @mention each get an inbox notification, plus email or push according to their settings. Nobody is notified twice about the same comment. Reply emails are properly threaded, and answering the email posts your response to the discussion.

A comment's lifecycle:
> **Posted** → optionally **Edited** (within 6 hours; an "edited" label appears if you edit more than a minute after posting) → stays up, or: **Deleted by you** (within 14 days, undeletable), **Removed by a moderator** (with a public reason), or **Disowned by you** (after 14 days — the comment stays but is no longer attributed to you)

## Common Questions

**Q: How long do I have to edit or delete my comment?**
A: You can edit for 6 hours after posting (or after your last edit) and delete for 14 days. After 14 days you can no longer delete, but you can disown the comment so it's no longer attributed to your account. These windows are fixed.

**Q: Why was my comment rejected with a message like "Please just upvote the parent post instead."?**
A: The site automatically declines low-effort comments — "me too", "nice", "+1", "this", "tl;dr", thread bumps, quote-only and emoji-only comments. Your upvote carries the same signal without the noise.

**Q: Why can't I see a comment's score?**
A: Scores are hidden on comments less than 36 hours old while their score is between −2 and +4, to keep early voting honest. You also won't see the score of a comment you've flagged. Moderators always see scores.

**Q: Why do I have to wait before commenting again in a thread?**
A: Threads that are collecting flags enforce a cooling-off period: the wait starts at about 2 minutes and grows with the number of flags in that reply chain — and grows faster if some of those flags are your own. You'll see exactly how long until your next comment is allowed.

**Q: Why can't I flag this comment?**
A: Flags are limited: not your own comments, not comments older than 7 days, not comments already down at −10, and not removed comments. A reason is always required. Note that after flagging a comment you can't reply to it — you've handed it to the moderators.

**Q: Who sees who flagged a comment?**
A: If your comment is flagged, you see how many flags of each kind it received — never who flagged it. Only moderators can see flaggers' names.

**Q: How deep can a discussion go?**
A: Replies nest up to 18 levels. Past that, the thread stops accepting replies ("Max reply depth reached.") — historically that's where discussions go off the rails.

**Q: What happens if a moderator removes my comment?**
A: The comment shows "Comment removed by moderator" with the moderator's name and their reason, and you lose karma in proportion to the votes the comment had received. Only a moderator can restore a moderator-removed comment; your own deletions you can undelete yourself.

**Q: Can I reply by email?**
A: Yes. Reply notification emails are threaded like a mailing list, and answering the email posts your reply to the discussion (such comments are marked as e-mailed).

**Q: How do I keep up with discussions site-wide?**
A: The newest-comments page lists every new comment, 20 per page, with a "Last Read" line marking where you left off. There's a public RSS feed (refreshed about every 2 minutes) and a private personalized feed that respects your tag filters. You can also revisit every comment you've upvoted, and your own recent threads.

## Tips & Best Practices

- **Preview before posting** — edits after the first minute permanently add an "edited" label.
- **Upvote instead of writing "+1"** — agreement-only comments are declined automatically anyway.
- **Wear a hat when speaking in an official role** — choose it from the "Put on hat:" selector in the comment box; note that uses of some official hats are publicly logged.
- **Flag or reply — not both.** Once you flag a comment, the site won't let you reply to it. Decide whether to engage or to leave it to the moderators.
- **Hide a story to silence it** — you'll stop receiving email and push notifications about its comments, while inbox notifications quietly accumulate in case you return.
- **Share comments with their permanent link** — it keeps working even when stories are merged together.

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- The 6-hour edit, 14-day delete, and 7-day flag windows cannot be extended.
- Threads stop at 18 levels of nesting — no exceptions.
- Disowning a comment is permanent: it stays public, attributed to an inactive account.
- Deleted and moderator-removed comments remain visible as placeholders ("Comment removed by author", "Comment removed by moderator …"); moderators can still read the original text. Comments from banned users show "Comment from banned user removed".
- The upvoted-comments page currently does not filter out stories you've hidden.
- Notification emails can occasionally fail silently — your site inbox is the reliable record.
- Old stories eventually stop accepting comments ("Story is no longer accepting comments.").
- The newest-comments feed always offers a next page, even when you've reached the end.

## Related Features

- **Stories** — every comment belongs to a story's discussion; stories close to comments as they age, and merged stories merge their discussions
- **Voting** — the same voting system powers story and comment scores
- **Moderation** — removals are publicly logged with reasons; deep-thread and flag activity is visible to moderators
- **Hats** — the role badges you can wear on comments
- **Inbox & Notifications** — where reply and mention alerts collect
- **Tag Filters & Hidden Stories** — both trim what appears in your newest-comments feed
