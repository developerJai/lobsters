# Comments — User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for using Comments.

---

## Prerequisites

- You must be logged in to post, reply, vote, or flag
- To wear a hat on a comment, a hat (role or affiliation badge) must already be assigned to your account
- To receive reply or mention alerts by email or phone push, enable those options in your notification settings first
- The story you're commenting on must still be accepting comments (older stories eventually close)

---

## Overview

This guide walks you through the full life of a comment: posting and previewing, replying in threads, editing, deleting, undeleting and disowning, voting and flagging, and keeping up with discussions site-wide. It also lists the fixed time windows that govern each action, and what to do when an action is refused.

---

## Task 1: Post a comment

### Steps

1. Open the story you want to discuss and go to its comment box
2. Write your comment
3. (Optional) Preview it first to see exactly how it will appear
4. (Optional) Choose a hat from the **Put on hat:** selector if you're speaking in an official role
5. Submit the comment

### Expected Result

Your comment appears in the story's discussion thread. If you accidentally submit the same comment twice, the site quietly keeps just one — you won't see a duplicate. If the comment was declined, see Troubleshooting below: very short agreement comments ("+1", "me too"), "this", "tl;dr", thread bumps, quote-only and emoji-only comments are rejected automatically.

---

## Task 2: Reply in a thread

### Steps

1. Find the comment you want to answer and start a reply on it
2. Write your reply (preview if you like) and submit

### Expected Result

Your reply appears nested directly under the comment it answers. The best-received replies in each group rise to the top automatically, and the ordering is stable between page loads. The person you replied to gets a notification, as does the story's author if they follow their story. Remember:

- Threads nest at most **18 levels** — past that, you'll see "Max reply depth reached." and the thread stops accepting replies.
- You **cannot reply to a comment you've flagged**.
- If the comment you were answering was removed while you typed, your reply won't go through.

---

## Task 3: Edit your comment

### Steps

1. Within **6 hours** of posting (or of your last edit), open your comment's edit option
2. Make your changes and submit

### Expected Result

The updated comment replaces the original. If you edited more than a minute after posting, an **"edited"** label is permanently added so readers know. After 6 hours, editing is no longer available — the window cannot be extended.

---

## Task 4: Delete, undelete, or disown your comment

### Steps — Delete (within 14 days)

1. Open your comment and choose delete
2. The comment is replaced in the thread by the placeholder "Comment removed by author"

### Steps — Undelete

1. On a comment you deleted yourself, choose undelete
2. The comment is restored

Note: only a moderator can restore a comment that a *moderator* removed.

### Steps — Disown (after 14 days)

1. On a comment older than 14 days, the delete option is no longer offered; choose disown instead
2. Confirm

### Expected Result

A disowned comment stays public but is attributed to an inactive account instead of you. **Disowning is permanent.**

---

## Task 5: Vote and flag

### Steps — Upvote

1. Upvote the comment; to change your mind, take the vote back

### Steps — Flag

1. Choose flag on the comment
2. Select a reason — a reason is always required

### Expected Result

Votes feed each comment's score. Flagging reports the comment to moderators. Flagging rules:

- You can't flag your own comments, comments older than **7 days**, comments already down at **−10**, or removed comments
- After flagging a comment, you can no longer see its score or reply to it
- The comment's author will see how many flags of each kind were received — never who flagged; only moderators see flaggers' names

Heavily downvoted comments (score −5 and below) appear collapsed in threads. Scores on comments under 36 hours old stay hidden while near zero (between −2 and +4).

---

## Task 6: Keep up with discussions

### Steps

1. Open the **newest comments** page to see every new comment site-wide, 20 per page — the **"Last Read"** line marks where you left off last visit
2. Subscribe by RSS: a public feed (refreshed about every 2 minutes) or your private personalized feed, which respects your tag filters
3. Visit your **upvoted comments** page to revisit every comment you've upvoted
4. Open your **recent threads** to re-read the discussions you took part in

### Expected Result

You can follow conversations without reopening each story. Tag filters and hidden stories trim what appears in your newest-comments feed. Note: the newest-comments feed always offers a next page, even when you've actually reached the end, and the upvoted-comments page currently does not filter out stories you've hidden.

---

## Variations

### Replying by email

When a reply notification arrives by email, you can simply answer the email — your response is posted to the discussion and marked as e-mailed. The emails are threaded like a mailing list, so the conversation reads correctly in your mail client.

### Commenting with a hat

Use the **Put on hat:** selector in the comment box when speaking in an official role. Be aware that uses of some official hats are publicly logged.

### Muting one story's discussion

Hide the story. Email and push notifications about its comments stop, while inbox notifications quietly accumulate in case you return to it later.

---

## Settings & Configuration

Notification preferences (in your account settings):

| Setting | Description | Default |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Reply notifications by email | Email when someone replies to your comment or story | Off until enabled |
| Mention notifications by email | Email when someone @mentions you — never doubled with a reply email for the same comment | Off until enabled |
| Pushover push notifications | The same reply/mention alerts on your phone | Off until enabled |
| Tag filters | Tags you filter also drop out of your newest-comments feed | None |
| Hidden stories | Hiding a story silences its comment emails/pushes | — |

Inbox notifications are always delivered regardless of these settings.

## Troubleshooting

### My comment was declined with a short message

**Cause:** The site automatically rejects low-effort comments: "me too", "nice", "+1", "this", "tl;dr", thread bumps, quote-only and emoji-only comments.
**Solution:** Add substance and repost — or simply upvote the parent comment, which carries the same signal.

### I'm told to wait before commenting again

**Cause:** The thread is collecting flags, so a cooling-off period applies — starting around 2 minutes and growing with the number of flags in the reply chain (faster if some flags are your own).
**Solution:** Wait the stated time; the message tells you exactly when your next comment is allowed.

### I can't edit or delete my comment

**Cause:** The fixed windows have passed — 6 hours for editing, 14 days for deleting — or a moderator removed the comment.
**Solution:** None for editing. For old comments, disown is offered instead of delete. Moderator-removed comments can only be restored by a moderator.

### I can't flag a comment

**Cause:** It's your own comment, it's older than 7 days, its score is already at −10, or it was removed.
**Solution:** These limits are by design. If something old still needs attention, contact the moderators directly.

### I can't reply to a comment

**Cause:** You've flagged it, the thread reached the 18-level depth limit, the comment was removed, or the story stopped accepting comments.
**Solution:** Unflag if you'd rather engage than report; for depth-capped or closed discussions, consider submitting a new story.

### A notification email never arrived

**Cause:** Email alerts are disabled in your settings, you've hidden the story, or the email failed silently.
**Solution:** Check your settings and hidden stories. Your site inbox is the reliable record — the notification will be there.

### A comment shows only a removal placeholder

**Cause:** "Comment removed by author" — the author deleted it; "Comment removed by moderator …" — removed with a publicly logged reason; "Comment from banned user removed" — the author was banned.
**Solution:** Nothing to do; moderators can still read the original if follow-up is needed.
