# Commenting on Lobsters

Everything you need to know about joining discussions: posting comments, replying, voting, flagging, and staying on top of replies.

## What are comments?

Every story on the site has a discussion section where logged-in members can post comments. Replies nest underneath the comment they respond to, forming threads, and the best-regarded comments in each group of siblings float to the top automatically.

## Posting a comment

Open a story and type into the comment box at the bottom, then press **Post**. You can press **Preview** first to see exactly how your comment will look before it goes live. Comments support Markdown formatting (a formatting help panel appears under the box).

A few things to know:

- You must be logged in to comment.
- If you own any hats (special badges for affiliations or roles), you can choose to "put on" one with your comment using the hat selector next to the buttons.
- Your comment starts with one point — your own automatic upvote.
- Older stories eventually stop accepting comments. If a story is about to close, the comment box warns you how many minutes remain.

### Comments the site will refuse

To keep discussions substantive, the site automatically rejects certain low-value comments, including: comments that just say "this", "me too", "nice", or "+1" (upvote the parent instead), "tl;dr"-style dismissals, thread "bumps", comments containing only a quote of someone else, comments made only of emoji, and empty comments.

## Replying and thread depth

Click **reply** under any comment to respond to it. Replies can nest up to 18 levels deep. Beyond that the reply link stops working and you'll see a notice suggesting the conversation has run its course — at that depth, discussions historically go off the rails.

Two guardrails apply to replies:

- **You can't reply to a comment you've flagged.** You've told the moderators about it; let them handle it.
- **Heated threads slow down.** If a thread is accumulating flags, the site enforces a short waiting period between your comments in that thread (starting at about two minutes and growing with the number of flags). You'll be told how long until your next comment is allowed.

## Editing and deleting

- **Edit**: You can edit your own comment for up to **6 hours** after posting (or after your last edit). If you edit more than a minute after posting, the comment is labeled "edited".
- **Delete**: You can delete your own comment for up to **14 days**. Deleted comments show as "Comment removed by author" — the text is gone for everyone except moderators.
- **Disown**: After 14 days you can no longer delete, but you can *disown* a comment — it stays visible but is no longer attributed to your account.
- **Undelete**: If you deleted your own comment (and a moderator didn't), you can undelete it.

## Voting and flagging

- **Upvote** a comment by clicking the arrow next to it; click again to take your vote back.
- **Flag** a comment to report it to moderators. You must pick a reason from the list. Flags are limited: you can't flag your own comments, comments older than **7 days**, or comments whose score has already dropped to −10.
- Heavily flagged comments (score of −5 or below) appear **collapsed** so casual readers skip them; you can expand them if you want.
- Comment scores are **hidden for the first 36 hours** when they're near zero (between −2 and +4). This reduces pile-on voting while a discussion is young. If you flag a comment, its score is hidden from you too.
- If your own comment gets flagged, you can see how many flags of each kind it received — but not who flagged it.

## Notifications: knowing when someone replies

You get a notification in your inbox when:

- someone **replies** to your comment,
- someone replies to **your story** (if you're following it), or
- someone **mentions** you by username (with an @ before your name).

Depending on your settings, you can also receive these as **email** or **Pushover** push notifications. Reply emails come threaded so they read like a conversation in your mail client, and you can reply to the email itself to post your response. If you've hidden a story, you won't get emails or pushes about its comments (though notifications quietly accumulate in case you unhide it). You won't be notified about mentions in a comment that already notified you as a reply — no duplicates.

Unread replies in a thread you've been reading are marked **(unread)** in the discussion.

## Finding comments

- **Newest comments** — the `/comments` page lists the latest comments across the whole site, 20 per page, with a "Last Read" line marking where you left off last visit. Available as an RSS feed, including a private feed personalized to your tag filters.
- **Upvoted comments** — a page listing every comment you've upvoted.
- **Your threads** — a page collecting the recent discussions you've participated in, shown as full threads.
- Every comment has a **permanent short link** that always takes you to its place in the story discussion, even if stories were merged.

Comments on stories you've hidden, or tagged with tags you filter, won't appear in your newest-comments feed.

## Moderation

Moderators can remove comments that break the rules. A removed comment shows "Comment removed by moderator" with the moderator's name and reason. Comments from banned users show "Comment from banned user removed". Moderator removals are logged publicly, and removal costs the author karma proportional to the votes the comment had received.
