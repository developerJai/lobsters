# Comments

> A comprehensive guide to commenting and discussions on Lobsters.

---

## What is Comments?

Comments are how the Lobsters community discusses submitted stories. Comments support threaded (nested) replies, markdown formatting, upvoting, flagging, and live preview. The system is designed to promote constructive conversation through quality-based sorting, rate limiting in heated threads, and depth limits.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Post threaded (nested) comments on any story
- Reply to specific comments, creating conversation threads
- Preview your comment with markdown formatting before posting
- Upvote helpful comments or flag problematic ones
- Edit your comments within 6 hours of posting
- Delete your comments within 14 days, or disown them after that
- Receive notifications when someone replies to you or mentions your username
- Wear a "hat" (verified flair) when commenting in an official capacity
- Track which comments are new since your last visit
- Browse the "Newest Comments" page to see recent activity across all stories

---

## How It Works

1. Navigate to a story and scroll to the comment box at the bottom.
2. Write your comment using markdown formatting. Click "Preview" to see how it will look.
3. Optionally select a hat if you want to comment in an official capacity.
4. Click "Post" to submit your comment.
5. Your comment appears in the thread, sorted by a confidence-based algorithm that promotes well-received comments.
6. Other users can upvote or flag your comment. You will see your score unless it is very new.
7. When someone replies to your comment or mentions your @username, you receive a notification in your inbox and optionally by email or push notification.
8. You can edit your comment for up to 6 hours after posting.
9. Comments nest up to 18 levels deep. If you reach the maximum depth, you are encouraged to start a new top-level comment.

## Common Questions

**Q: How are comments sorted within a thread?**
A: Comments are sorted using a confidence-based algorithm (similar to Reddit's "best" sort). Comments with more upvotes and fewer flags appear higher, but newer comments with fewer votes are not immediately pushed to the bottom.

**Q: Why do I see "speed limit reached" when trying to reply?**
A: In heated threads with many flags, the system slows down how quickly you can post follow-up replies. The delay increases based on the number of flags in the conversation. This is designed to cool down contentious discussions. Moderators are exempt.

**Q: Why can I not see the score on a comment?**
A: Scores on very recent comments (or those near zero) are hidden from non-moderators to discourage meta-discussion about voting. Once a comment is older or has a more definitive score, it becomes visible.

**Q: What are the flag reasons for comments?**
A: You can flag a comment as Off-topic, Me-too, Troll, Unkind, or Spam. You must have earned enough reputation (karma) to flag comments.

**Q: Why was my comment rejected?**
A: The system filters out certain low-effort comments including "this," "me too," "+1," quote-only replies, emoji-only comments, and comments that consist of spaced-out capital letters. Comments on stories that have closed for discussion (after 90 days) are also rejected.

**Q: Can I reply to a deleted comment?**
A: No. You cannot reply to comments that have been deleted or moderated.

**Q: What does "disown" mean?**
A: After 14 days, you can no longer delete a comment, but you can "disown" it. This transfers authorship to a generic account while keeping the comment visible, removing your name from it.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Add substance to your comments; the system filters out very short or low-effort replies
- Use markdown for code blocks, links, and emphasis to make your comments clearer
- Preview your comment before posting to catch formatting mistakes
- If a thread is getting heated, take a break -- the speed limiter will slow you down anyway
- Use the @username mention to notify specific people in your comment
- Wear a hat when speaking in an official capacity to add credibility and transparency

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Comments close on stories after 90 days
- You can only edit comments for 6 hours after posting
- You can only delete comments within 14 days (disown after that)
- Maximum nesting depth is 18 levels
- You need a minimum karma level (50) to flag comments
- Comments cannot be flagged after 7 days or below a score of -10
- Comments scoring -5 or lower are automatically collapsed
- The comment body minimum is not just a character count -- low-effort patterns are specifically detected and rejected
