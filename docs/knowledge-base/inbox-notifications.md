# Inbox & Notifications

> A comprehensive guide to your inbox and notification system on Lobsters.

---

## What is Inbox & Notifications?

Your inbox is a single place where all notifications directed at you are collected. This includes replies to your comments, mentions of your username, private messages, and moderator mail. The inbox shows both read and unread notifications, with an unread count badge in the site header so you always know when something new has arrived.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Unified inbox combining comment replies, mentions, private messages, and moderator mail
- Unread notification count displayed as a badge in the site header
- Separate views for "All" notifications and "Unread" only
- Automatic read-tracking: viewing your inbox marks displayed notifications as read
- Smart filtering that suppresses low-quality notifications (from flagged content, hidden stories, or filtered tags)
- User preference to enable or disable mention notifications
- Pagination on the "All" view (25 notifications per page)

---

## How It Works

1. When someone replies to your comment, mentions your username (with @ or ~), sends you a private message, or includes you in a moderator mail thread, a notification is created in your inbox.
2. The unread count badge in the site header updates to show how many new notifications you have.
3. Click the inbox link to view your notifications. If you have unread items, you are taken to the "Unread" view. If everything is read, you see the "All" view.
4. Viewing your inbox automatically marks the displayed notifications as read. The badge count updates on the next page load.
5. The system automatically filters out low-quality notifications. For example, you will not be notified about replies on stories you have hidden, content with tags you have filtered out, or comments that have been heavily flagged or deleted.
6. You can control whether @-mention notifications appear in your inbox from your Settings page. Reply notifications are always delivered.
7. In addition to inbox notifications, you may also receive email or push notifications depending on your settings.

## Common Questions

**Q: What types of notifications appear in my inbox?**
A: Four types: replies to your comments, @-mentions of your username in comments, private messages from other users, and moderator mail messages.

**Q: Why am I not seeing a notification for a reply or mention?**
A: The system filters out notifications for low-quality or unwanted content. This includes replies on stories you have hidden, comments that have been deleted or heavily flagged, stories with tags you have filtered out, and replies from users whose comments you have flagged on the same story.

**Q: How do I stop receiving mention notifications?**
A: Go to your Settings page and disable the "Inbox mentions" option. You will still receive reply notifications.

**Q: Why does the unread badge not update immediately after I view my inbox?**
A: Notifications are marked as read after the page is rendered. The badge count will be correct on your next page load.

**Q: Can I see old notifications?**
A: Yes. The "All" view in your inbox shows all notifications (both read and unread), paginated at 25 per page.

**Q: Do I get notified about my own comments?**
A: No. You are not notified about your own comments, even if they are replies to your own content.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Check your inbox regularly to stay engaged with conversations
- Use the "Unread" view to quickly see what needs your attention
- If you are getting too many mention notifications, you can disable them in Settings
- Remember that filtering tags and hiding stories also suppresses related notifications, keeping your inbox focused

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- You must be logged in to access your inbox
- The "Unread" view loads all unread notifications without pagination, so it may be slow if you have many unread items
- Notifications for low-quality content are automatically suppressed and cannot be recovered
- The unread count badge updates on the page load after you view your inbox, not immediately
- Email and push notification delivery depends on your settings and may fail silently in rare cases
