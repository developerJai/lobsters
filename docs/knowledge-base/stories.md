# Stories

> A comprehensive guide to submitting, browsing, and interacting with stories on Lobsters.

---

## What is Stories?

Stories are the core content on Lobsters. A story is a link to an interesting article, project, or discussion -- or a text post written directly on the site. Users submit stories, and the community votes, comments, and suggests improvements to help the best content rise to the top.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Submit stories with a URL or as a text post
- Automatic title extraction when you paste a URL
- Upvote stories you find valuable
- Flag stories that are off-topic, already posted, broken, or spam
- Hide stories you do not want to see
- Save stories to read later (bookmarking)
- Suggest better tags or titles for any story
- Community-driven tag and title suggestions that auto-apply when enough people agree
- Duplicate URL detection warns you if a link has already been submitted
- Stories generate a preview image for social media sharing

---

## How It Works

1. Click "Submit Story" and paste a URL or write a text post.
2. The site automatically fetches the page title from your URL. You can edit it.
3. Choose one or more tags that describe the topic (at least one non-media tag is required).
4. Optionally add a description with additional context.
5. If the URL was recently submitted, you will be warned about the duplicate.
6. After submission, your story appears on the "Newest" page and may reach the front page based on votes and engagement.
7. Other users can upvote your story, flag it, suggest better tags or titles, and post comments.
8. If two or more users suggest the same tag change, it is automatically applied.
9. You can edit your story within 6 hours of submission. After that, only moderators can make changes.
10. Stories accept comments for 90 days after submission.

## Common Questions

**Q: How does a story reach the front page?**
A: Stories are ranked by a "hotness" algorithm that considers upvotes, comment activity, the age of the story, and tag modifiers. Stories with more engagement rise higher. Author-submitted content gets a small boost.

**Q: Can I delete my own story?**
A: Yes, you can delete your story as long as it has not been moderated. After 28 days, the delete option changes to "disown," which transfers authorship to a generic inactive account while keeping the story visible.

**Q: What happens when I flag a story?**
A: You must choose a reason (Off-topic, Already Posted, Broken Link, or Spam). Flags reduce a story's score. Stories can only be flagged within 14 days of submission and you cannot flag your own stories.

**Q: Why was my submission blocked?**
A: New users face some restrictions: you cannot submit links to domains the site has not seen before, link to bug trackers or discussion threads (anti-brigading), or use certain restricted tags. In these cases, a conversation is started between you, the person who invited you, and the moderators.

**Q: Can I suggest a better title for someone else's story?**
A: Yes. Click "Suggest" on any story to propose new tags or a different title. If enough users (currently 2) suggest the same change, it is applied automatically.

**Q: How long can I edit my story after posting?**
A: You have 6 hours to edit your story. After that, only moderators can make changes.

**Q: What does "saving" a story do?**
A: Saving bookmarks the story so you can find it later under your "Saved" page. It does not affect the story's score or visibility.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Write descriptive, accurate titles -- avoid clickbait and all-caps
- Choose tags carefully; well-tagged stories help people find relevant content
- Mark yourself as the author if you wrote the linked content (this gives a small ranking boost and is appreciated for transparency)
- Add a brief description for context, especially for dense or technical content
- Check for duplicates before submitting by using the search feature
- Do not include tracking parameters in URLs (they are automatically stripped)

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Titles cannot contain emoji or be entirely uppercase
- Stories require at least one non-media tag
- New users cannot use certain restricted tags
- URL submissions are checked for duplicates within the last 30 days
- Comments close automatically 90 days after submission
- Stories from banned domains or origins are rejected
- Editing the URL is only possible within 6 hours unless you are a moderator
