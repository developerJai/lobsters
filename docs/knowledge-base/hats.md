# Hats

> A comprehensive guide to the verified flair system on Lobsters.

---

## What is Hats?

Hats are a verified flair system that lets you speak on behalf of a project, organization, or company when posting comments or sending private messages. Each hat is a small badge that appears next to your name (like "OpenBSD Developer" or "Mozilla Employee"), so the community knows when someone is commenting in an official capacity rather than as a private individual.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Request a new hat by providing its name, a verification link, and a private note for moderators
- Moderators review and approve or reject hat requests
- Wear a hat when posting a comment or sending a private message to indicate you are speaking officially
- Hold multiple hats but wear only one at a time on a given comment or message
- Retire ("doff") a hat you no longer need, with a reason that is logged publicly
- View a public directory of all active hats on the site

---

## How It Works

1. To request a hat, go to the hat request page and fill in the hat name (e.g., "Django Contributor"), a verification link (such as your organization's team page or a commit showing your membership), and a private comment explaining why you qualify.
2. Your request is reviewed by a moderator. They may adjust the hat name or link before approving.
3. If approved, you receive a private message confirming the hat has been granted. The hat becomes available for you to wear.
4. When writing a comment or message, you can select which hat to wear from a dropdown. The hat badge appears next to your name on that comment or message.
5. If you no longer need a hat (for example, you left an organization), you can doff (retire) it. You must provide a reason, which is logged in the public moderation log.
6. Moderators can also doff hats on behalf of users, or edit a hat's name and link.
7. The public hats directory lists all active hats grouped by hat name, so anyone can see who holds which hats.

## Common Questions

**Q: What is the purpose of hats?**
A: Hats provide transparency. When someone comments wearing a hat, the community knows they are speaking in an official capacity for a project or organization, not just sharing a personal opinion.

**Q: How do I request a hat?**
A: Go to the hat request page (linked from the site navigation or hats directory). Fill in the hat name, a link that verifies your affiliation, and a private note explaining your connection. A moderator will review your request.

**Q: Can I have more than one hat?**
A: Yes. You can hold multiple hats, but you can only wear one at a time on any given comment or message.

**Q: What does it mean to "doff" a hat?**
A: Doffing a hat means retiring it. After doffing, the hat no longer appears as an option when you write comments or messages. Previous comments where you wore that hat still show it.

**Q: What happens to my old comments if a hat is renamed?**
A: It depends on how the moderator makes the change. If they edit the hat in place, all your old comments show the new name. If they retire the old hat and create a new one, your old comments keep the original hat name and the new name applies only to future comments.

**Q: Can anyone see the hats directory?**
A: Yes. The hats directory is public. However, for logged-out visitors, email-based verification links show only the domain (not the full email address) for privacy.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Only request hats for affiliations you can genuinely verify
- Provide a clear verification link (your organization's team page, a public commit, or a company profile) to speed up approval
- Wear your hat when sharing insider knowledge or representing your project, and leave it off for casual personal comments
- Doff a hat promptly if your affiliation changes, to maintain community trust

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Hat requests must include a verification link and a comment for moderators
- Only moderators can approve or reject hat requests
- Once a hat is doffed, it cannot be un-doffed (a new hat must be created)
- All hat grants, doffs, and edits are logged publicly in the moderation log
- You must be logged in to request, wear, or doff a hat
