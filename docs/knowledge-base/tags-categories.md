# Tags & Categories

> A comprehensive guide to how content is classified and filtered on Lobsters.

---

## What is Tags & Categories?

Tags and categories form the content classification system on Lobsters. Every story must be tagged with one or more labels that describe its topic (like "programming," "security," or "linux"). Tags are grouped into broader categories (like "platforms" or "culture"). You can filter out tags you are not interested in, so your feed only shows topics you care about.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Every story is labeled with one or more tags describing its topic
- Tags are organized into categories for easier browsing
- Filter out tags you do not want to see in your feeds
- Browse all stories for a specific tag or combination of tags
- Browse all stories in a category
- Discover related tags based on what topics frequently appear together
- Administrators can create, edit, and retire tags and categories

---

## How It Works

1. When submitting a story, choose one or more tags that describe its topic. At least one non-media tag is required.
2. Tags appear as colored labels on each story in the feed.
3. To filter out topics you do not want to see, visit the "Filters" page from the navigation menu.
4. Check the tags you want to hide, then save your preferences.
5. Filtered stories will no longer appear in your feeds (homepage, newest, etc.).
6. Logged-in users have their filters saved to their account. Visitors who are not logged in have filters saved in a browser cookie.
7. You can browse a single tag by clicking on it, or combine multiple tags by visiting URLs like `/t/tag1,tag2`.
8. You can also browse by category to see all stories that fall under a group of related tags.

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I filter out topics I am not interested in?**
A: Go to the "Filters" page (linked in the site navigation). Check the boxes next to any tags you want to hide, then click "Save." Those topics will be removed from your feeds.

**Q: Do my filters persist if I am not logged in?**
A: Yes, but they are stored in a browser cookie. If you clear your cookies or switch browsers, you will lose your filter settings. Logging in saves your filters permanently to your account.

**Q: Can I search by tag?**
A: Yes. You can click on any tag to see all stories with that tag. You can also use the search feature with `tag:tagname` to find stories with a specific tag.

**Q: Why can I not see certain tags when submitting a story?**
A: Some tags are restricted to moderators only (privileged tags). Additionally, new users may be prevented from using certain tags until their account matures.

**Q: What is the difference between a tag and a category?**
A: A tag is a specific label (like "rust" or "privacy"). A category is a broader group that contains related tags (like "programming" or "culture"). You filter by individual tags, but you can browse by either tags or categories.

**Q: Can I suggest new tags?**
A: Tags can only be created by site administrators. If you think a new tag is needed, you can discuss it with the community or contact a moderator.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Tag your stories accurately -- good tagging helps others find relevant content
- Use the filter system liberally to customize your feed to your interests
- Check the tag descriptions on the Tags page if you are unsure which tag to use
- Browse related tags on a tag's page to discover adjacent topics
- Remember that media tags (like "video" or "pdf") are supplementary and do not count as primary tags

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Every story must have at least one non-media tag
- New users may not be able to use all tags
- Privileged tags are restricted to moderators
- Filtering a privileged tag requires moderator status
- Anonymous users lose their filter settings if they clear their cookies
- All tag and category changes by administrators are logged publicly in the moderation log
