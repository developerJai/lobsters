# Mod Mail

> A comprehensive guide to the moderator mail system on Lobsters.

---

## What is Mod Mail?

Mod Mail is a group messaging system used for private conversations between moderators and users. It supports threaded discussions with a subject line and multiple recipients. Mod mail is also used automatically when new users trigger certain submission rules -- the system creates a conversation between the new user, their inviter, and a system account so the inviter can help mentor the newcomer.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Moderators can create new mod mail threads with a subject, recipients, and optional references to specific stories or comments
- Recipients and moderators can view and reply to threads
- Automatic mod mail creation when a new user's story submission triggers a community guideline (unseen domain, restricted link type, or restricted tag)
- Notifications delivered in-app, by email, and optionally via push notification
- Mod mail messages appear in your unified inbox alongside other notifications

---

## How It Works

1. A moderator creates a new mod mail thread by entering a subject, selecting recipients (by username), and optionally referencing specific stories or comments for context.
2. All recipients see the thread in their mod mail section and receive notifications in their inbox.
3. Any recipient or moderator can reply to the thread. Replies are visible to all participants.
4. When a new user submits a story that triggers a guideline check (for example, linking to a domain the site has not seen before), the system automatically creates a mod mail thread between the new user, their inviter, and the system. This gives the inviter an opportunity to mentor the new user about site guidelines.
5. Each new reply sends notifications to all other participants via their inbox, and by email or push notification depending on their settings.
6. Moderators can edit thread metadata (subject, recipients, and references) after creation.

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Who can see my mod mail threads?**
A: Only the recipients listed on the thread and site moderators can see it. Mod mail is not visible to the general public.

**Q: Why did I receive a mod mail I did not expect?**
A: If you are a new user, the system may have automatically created a mod mail thread because your story submission triggered a guideline. If you are an inviter, you may have been included because someone you invited triggered a guideline. Read the message for details about what happened.

**Q: Can I reply to a mod mail thread?**
A: Yes. If you are a recipient of the thread, you can reply from the thread page. Your reply is visible to all other participants.

**Q: How is mod mail different from private messages?**
A: Private messages are one-to-one conversations between two users. Mod mail supports multiple recipients and is primarily used for moderator-to-user or moderator-to-moderator communication. Mod mail threads can also reference specific stories or comments for context.

**Q: Will I be notified about new mod mail messages?**
A: Yes. New mod mail messages appear in your inbox. You may also receive email or push notifications depending on your settings.

---

## Tips & Best Practices

- Read mod mail messages promptly, especially if they relate to a story you submitted
- If you are an inviter and receive a mod mail about someone you invited, take the opportunity to help them understand the site's guidelines
- Reply within the thread to keep the conversation organized for all participants
- If you are a moderator, use story and comment references to provide context in mod mail threads

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Only moderators can create new mod mail threads
- Mod mail threads cannot be deleted
- You must be a recipient or a moderator to view a mod mail thread
- Message replies must be at least 20 characters long
- Any logged-in user who knows a thread identifier could potentially reply to it, even if they are not a listed recipient
