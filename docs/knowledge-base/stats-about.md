# Stats & About

> A comprehensive guide to the informational and statistical pages on Lobsters.

---

## What is Stats & About?

Lobsters provides several public pages that give visibility into the site's activity, policies, and community. The stats page shows monthly graphs of site growth and activity. The about page describes the site's philosophy, guidelines, and features. There is also a chat page with community chat details and a privacy policy page. The site logo subtly changes color based on real-time traffic activity.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Monthly activity graphs showing users joining, active users, stories submitted, comments posted, and votes cast
- Graphs show historical data from the site's founding to the current month
- Current month data is extrapolated to show projected totals
- About page with site description, community guidelines, topicality rules, tagging philosophy, invitation system explanation, and transparency commitments
- Chat page with information about the community IRC channel
- Privacy policy page
- Real-time traffic indicator through the site logo color (brighter red means more activity)

---

## How It Works

1. Visit the Stats page (linked from the site navigation under About) to see monthly activity graphs.
2. Each graph shows data from the site's founding through the current month. The current month's data point is an extrapolation based on activity so far this month.
3. The About page explains how the site works, including community guidelines, what makes content on-topic, how the tagging system works, how invitations work, what makes the moderation system transparent, and various site features.
4. The Chat page provides details on the community's IRC channel on Libera.Chat.
5. The Privacy page outlines the site's minimal data collection practices.
6. The site logo in the top-left corner changes color intensity based on real-time activity. A brighter red means more stories, comments, and votes are happening right now. On special dates (like certain holidays), the logo may display novelty colors.

## Common Questions

**Q: How often are the stats graphs updated?**
A: The graphs are cached and refreshed periodically (approximately every two days). They may not reflect the very latest activity.

**Q: What does the "active users" graph measure?**
A: Active users are counted by looking at who submitted stories, posted comments, or cast votes in each month. A user who did any of these activities at least once counts as active for that month.

**Q: What is the extrapolated data point on the graphs?**
A: For the current month, the graph shows a projected total based on how much activity has occurred so far. For example, if it is the 15th of the month and 500 stories have been submitted, the extrapolation estimates about 1,000 for the full month.

**Q: Where can I find the community guidelines?**
A: The About page contains detailed guidelines on what content is on-topic, how to tag stories, how the invitation system works, and what the community values.

**Q: What is the IRC channel?**
A: The Lobsters community has an IRC channel on Libera.Chat where members can chat in real time. Visit the Chat page for connection details.

**Q: Why does the site logo change color?**
A: The logo's red intensity reflects current site activity. More activity (votes, comments, stories) in the past 15 minutes means a brighter red. It is a subtle, fun indicator of how busy the site is right now.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Check the stats page to understand site growth trends and activity patterns
- Read the About page when you first join to understand community norms and what makes content on-topic
- Join the IRC channel to connect with other community members in real time
- The stats graphs are a good way to see how active the community is over time

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- Stats graphs may be up to two days behind current data
- The extrapolated data point for the current month is an estimate and may not be accurate
- The About page content is specific to the official Lobsters site; forks of the software must write their own
- The traffic intensity indicator is updated every few minutes, not in real time
- All stats and informational pages are publicly accessible without logging in
