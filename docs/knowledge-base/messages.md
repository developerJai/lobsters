# Messages

> A comprehensive guide to private messaging on Lobsters.

---

## What is Messages?

Messages are private one-to-one conversations between Lobsters users. You can send, receive, read, reply to, and delete private messages. Messages support markdown formatting and can optionally include a hat (verified flair) for official communication. You are notified about new messages through your inbox, and optionally by email or push notification.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Send and receive private messages to and from any user
- Reply to messages with auto-filled recipient and subject line
- Format messages with markdown (links, bold, code blocks, etc.)
- Attach a hat to a message when speaking in an official capacity
- Delete messages independently -- the other person's copy is not affected
- Batch-delete multiple messages at once
- Mark a message as unread ("Keep As New") to come back to it later
- Receive notifications for new messages in your inbox, by email, or via push notification

---

## How It Works

1. Go to your Messages page and use the compose form to write a new message. Enter the recipient's username, a subject line, and the message body.
2. If you hold any hats, you can optionally select one to attach to the message, indicating you are writing in an official capacity.
3. Click "Send" to deliver the message. The recipient will see it in their inbox.
4. When you receive a message, it appears in your inbox with an unread indicator. Click on it to read the full message.
5. From the message view, you can reply (the recipient and subject are pre-filled), delete the message, or mark it as unread to deal with it later.
6. Deleting a message only removes it from your view. The other person's copy remains until they delete it too. Once both sides have deleted a message, it is permanently removed.
7. You can select multiple messages and delete them in bulk from the inbox or sent views.
8. Notification delivery depends on the recipient's preferences: they will always see it in their inbox, and may also receive an email or push notification if they have those options enabled in their settings.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I send a message to any user?**
A: Yes, as long as you are logged in and know their exact username. The username lookup is case-sensitive.

**Q: What happens when I delete a message?**
A: Deleting a message only hides it from your view. The other person can still see their copy. The message is permanently removed only after both the sender and recipient have deleted it.

**Q: How long can a message be?**
A: Messages must be between 5 and 8,192 characters long.

**Q: Can I edit a message after sending it?**
A: No. Once sent, messages cannot be edited.

**Q: What does "Keep As New" do?**
A: It marks the message as unread again, so it appears as a new message in your inbox. This is useful when you want to come back to a message later.

**Q: How do I know if I have new messages?**
A: The unread notification count in the site header includes new messages. You will also receive email or push notifications if you have those enabled in your settings.

**Q: Can I attach a hat to a message?**
A: Yes. If you have any active hats, you can select one when composing a message. The hat badge will appear on the message, indicating you are writing in an official capacity.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Use clear subject lines so both you and the recipient can find the conversation later
- Use markdown formatting for code snippets, links, and emphasis
- Enable email or push notifications for messages in your Settings so you do not miss important conversations
- Use "Keep As New" to flag messages you need to follow up on

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- You must be logged in to send or receive messages
- Messages must be between 5 and 8,192 characters
- Messages cannot be edited after sending
- Deleting a message only removes your copy; the other person's copy is unaffected
- The system sends notifications based on the recipient's preferences, not the sender's
