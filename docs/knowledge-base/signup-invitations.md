# Signup & Invitations

> A comprehensive guide to joining Lobsters and inviting others.

---

## What is Signup & Invitations?

Lobsters is an invitation-only community. New users can only create an account by receiving an invitation from an existing member. This system creates a chain of accountability: every user is linked to the person who invited them. If a user causes problems, the inviter shares some responsibility. In some configurations, non-users can also request an invitation publicly, which existing members can then fulfill.

---

## Key Capabilities

- Create an account using an invitation link from an existing user
- Existing users can send invitations by email to people they want to invite
- Public invitation request system (when enabled) where non-users can request to join
- Email verification for invitation requests before they become visible to members
- Every user is linked to their inviter in the public invitation tree
- Moderators can disable or re-enable a user's ability to send invitations

---

## How It Works

1. An existing Lobsters user decides to invite someone and enters their email address and an optional message on the invitation form (found on the Settings page).
2. The invitee receives an email with a unique invitation link.
3. The invitee clicks the link, which takes them to the signup page where they create a username and password.
4. After signing up, the new user's profile is linked to their inviter in the public invitation tree.
5. If public invitation requests are enabled, anyone can visit the request page, enter their name, email, and a link to verify their identity (such as a personal website or GitHub profile).
6. After submitting a request, the requester must confirm their email by clicking a verification link.
7. Once verified, the request becomes visible to eligible existing users who can choose to send an invitation.
8. The inviter's invitation is then sent to the requester's email, and they follow the normal signup process.

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I get an account on Lobsters?**
A: You need an invitation from an existing user. Ask someone you know who is already a member, or if public invitation requests are enabled, submit a request on the site.

**Q: How do I invite someone?**
A: Go to your Settings page and use the invitation form. Enter the person's email address and an optional personal message, then click "Send."

**Q: Why can I not send invitations?**
A: There are a few requirements: your account must be older than 70 days, your invitation privileges must not have been disabled by a moderator, and your karma must be above -4. If any of these conditions are not met, you cannot send invitations.

**Q: What happens if I invite someone who causes problems?**
A: Lobsters tracks the invitation chain publicly. If someone you invited behaves poorly, your name is visible as their inviter. Moderators may also disable your invitation privileges if a pattern emerges.

**Q: Can I see who invited whom?**
A: Yes. The invitation tree is publicly visible under the "Users" page. It shows every user and who invited them.

**Q: What is an invitation request?**
A: When enabled, non-users can submit a request to join by providing their name, email, and a link to verify their identity. Existing users with sufficient karma can review these requests and send invitations to applicants they find credible.

**Q: What happens if my invitation link does not work?**
A: Invitation links can only be used once. If the link has already been redeemed or has been marked as used for any reason, you will see an "Invalid or expired invitation" error. Ask the person who invited you to send a new one.

## Tips & Best Practices

- Only invite people you trust to be constructive community members
- When requesting an invitation, provide a real verification link (your personal website, GitHub, or LinkedIn) to increase your chances
- Remember that your reputation is linked to the people you invite
- Check the invitation tree to see how the community is connected

---

## Limitations & Important Notes

- New users (accounts less than 70 days old) cannot send invitations
- Your invitation privileges can be disabled by moderators if you invite problematic users
- You need a minimum karma of -4 to send invitations
- To view and fulfill public invitation requests, you need at least 50 karma (or be a moderator)
- Each invitation link can only be used once
- If a logged-in user accidentally clicks an invitation link, the invitation is marked as used and the inviter must send a new one
