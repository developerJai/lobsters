# Hats -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for requesting, wearing, and removing hats on Lobsters.

---

## Prerequisites

- A registered Lobsters account (logged in)
- To request a hat: a verifiable connection to the project, company, or role the hat represents (such as a public profile page or project contributor page)

---

## Overview

Hats are pieces of verified flair that appear next to your username when you comment or send messages. They indicate that you have a verified connection to a particular project, company, or role. You can request a hat by providing proof of your affiliation, and moderators will review your request. Once granted, you can choose to wear a hat on individual comments and messages, or permanently remove a hat you no longer want.

---

## Task 1: Request a New Hat

### Steps

1. Click your username in the site header and then click **Hats** (or navigate directly to the hat request page).
2. In the **Hat** field, enter the name you want displayed (e.g., the name of a project or company).
3. In the **Link** field, enter a URL that proves your connection to the hat (e.g., a link to your name on a project's contributors page, your company profile, or a public directory listing).
4. In the **Comment** field, explain why you should receive this hat and how the link proves your affiliation.
5. Click **Request Hat**.

### Expected Result

A success message confirms your request has been submitted. Moderators will review your request and either approve or deny it. You will receive a notification when a decision is made.

---

## Task 2: Wear a Hat on a Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story where you want to leave a comment.
2. Click **Reply** to open the comment form.
3. Write your comment in the text area.
4. Click the **Put on hat** dropdown below the text area.
5. Select the hat you want to wear from the list of your approved hats.
6. Click **Post**.

### Expected Result

Your comment is posted with the selected hat displayed next to your username. Other users can see the hat, which indicates your verified affiliation with the project or organization.

---

## Task 3: Wear a Hat on a Private Message

### Steps

1. Navigate to the message compose form (either from your messages page or from a user's profile).
2. Fill in the **To**, **Subject**, and **Message** fields.
3. Click the **Put on hat** dropdown.
4. Select the hat you want to wear.
5. Click **Send Message**.

### Expected Result

Your message is sent with the selected hat shown next to your username. The recipient sees your verified affiliation.

---

## Task 4: Remove a Hat Permanently (Doff)

### Steps

1. Navigate to your profile page by clicking your username in the site header.
2. Find the hat you want to remove in your hats list.
3. Click the **Doff** link next to the hat.
4. On the confirmation page, enter a **Reason** for removing the hat.
5. Click **Doff Hat**.

### Expected Result

The hat is permanently removed from your account. You can no longer wear it on comments or messages. This action cannot be undone -- if you want the hat back, you must submit a new request.

---

## Variations

### Commenting Without a Hat

If you have hats but do not want to wear one on a particular comment, simply leave the **Put on hat** dropdown on its default setting (no hat selected). Your comment will appear with just your username.

### Multiple Hats

If you have been granted multiple hats, you can choose a different hat for each comment or message. Only one hat can be worn per comment or message.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Hat request was denied

**Cause:** The moderators could not verify your connection to the hat based on the link and comment you provided.
**Solution:** Submit a new request with a clearer link that directly proves your affiliation. For example, link to a page where your Lobsters username or real name appears as a contributor or employee.

### The "Put on hat" dropdown does not appear

**Cause:** You do not have any approved hats on your account.
**Solution:** Request a hat first through the hat request page. The dropdown only appears when you have at least one approved hat.

### A doffed hat still appears on old comments

**Cause:** Doffing a hat removes it from your account going forward, but comments you posted while wearing the hat retain it for historical accuracy.
**Solution:** This is expected behavior. The hat on past comments reflects the affiliation you had at the time you posted them.
