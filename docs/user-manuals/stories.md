# Stories -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for submitting, voting on, saving, and hiding stories.

---

## Prerequisites

- A registered Lobsters account (logged in)
- Karma of -4 or higher to submit stories
- Accounts less than 70 days old may have restrictions on certain tags and domains

---

## Overview

Stories are the core content on Lobsters. You can submit links or text posts, vote on stories from other users, save stories for later reading, and hide stories you do not want to see. Each story must have at least one tag and a title.

---

## Task 1: Submit a New Story

### Steps

1. Click **Submit Story** in the site header (or navigate to the submission page).
2. Enter the link in the **URL** field.
3. Click **Fetch Title** to automatically fill in the title from the linked page.
4. Review the **Title** field and edit if needed (remove site names, blog names, and author names from the title).
5. Select one or more **Tags** from the multi-select dropdown. At least one non-media tag is required.
6. Optionally, enter additional context in the **Text** field. For text-only posts (no URL), this field is required.
7. Check **I am the author of the story at this URL** if you wrote the linked content.
8. Check **Follow this story to receive replies via e-mail and Pushover** if you want notifications for comments.
9. Click **Preview** to see how your story will look before submitting.
10. Click **Submit** to publish the story.

### Expected Result

You are redirected to the story page. Your story appears on the **Newest** feed and, if it receives upvotes, on the front page.

---

## Task 2: Upvote a Story

### Steps

1. Locate the story you want to upvote on any feed page (home, newest, active, etc.).
2. Click the **score number** (or the upvote arrow) to the left of the story title.

### Expected Result

The score number increases by one and the story row highlights to indicate your upvote. Click the score again to remove your vote.

---

## Task 3: Flag a Story

### Steps

1. Locate the story you want to flag on any feed page.
2. Click the **flag** link in the story's byline area (below the title).
3. Select a reason from the dropdown that appears:
   - **Off-topic**
   - **Already Posted**
   - **Broken Link**
   - **Spam**
4. The flag is submitted automatically after selecting a reason.

### Expected Result

The story row updates to show a "flagged" state. To remove your flag, click **unflag** in the same location.

---

## Task 4: Save a Story for Later

### Steps

1. Locate the story you want to save on any feed page.
2. Click the **save** link in the story's byline area.

### Expected Result

The story is added to your saved stories list. The link changes to **unsave**. You can view all saved stories by navigating to **Saved** in the site header.

---

## Task 5: Hide a Story

### Steps

1. Locate the story you want to hide on any feed page.
2. Click the **hide** link in the story's byline area.

### Expected Result

The story disappears from your feeds. The link changes to **unhide**. You can view all hidden stories by navigating to **Hidden** in the site header. Click **unhide** to restore the story to your feeds.

---

## Task 6: Edit a Story You Submitted

### Steps

1. Find your story on any feed page or navigate to its story page.
2. Click the **edit** link in the story's byline area (available within 6 hours of submission).
3. Modify the **Title**, **Tags**, **Text**, or **URL** fields as needed.
4. Click **Save** to apply your changes.

### Expected Result

Your story is updated. If you need to delete the story, click **Delete** on the edit page and confirm when prompted.

---

## Task 7: Suggest Tag or Title Changes for Someone Else's Story

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page of a story submitted by another user.
2. Click the **suggest** link in the story's byline area.
3. On the suggestion form, modify the **Title** and/or **Tags** fields.
4. Click **Suggest Changes**.

### Expected Result

Your suggestion is recorded. If two or more users suggest the same change, the change is applied automatically.

---

## Variations

### Submitting a Text-Only Story

If you have no URL to link, leave the **URL** field empty and write your content in the **Text** field. This is common for "Ask" posts and discussion topics.

### Resubmitting a Previously Posted URL

If a URL was already submitted, you will see a warning with a link to the existing story. You can still submit if you believe a new discussion is warranted.

### Disowning a Story

If your story is older than 28 days, the **edit** page shows a **Disown** option. Disowning transfers authorship to the "inactive-user" account while preserving the story.

## Troubleshooting

### "is an unseen domain from a new user"

**Cause:** New accounts (less than 70 days old) cannot submit stories from domains the site has not seen before.
**Solution:** Wait until your account is older, or ask your inviter for guidance. A moderator conversation will be started automatically.

### Story does not appear on the front page despite having upvotes

**Cause:** The story's tags may have a negative hotness modifier, your tag filters may be hiding it, or the story's overall score is being pushed down by flags.
**Solution:** Check your tag filters at **Filters** in the site header. Stories with downward hotness modifiers on their tags need more upvotes to reach the front page.

### Cannot edit a story

**Cause:** The 6-hour editing window has passed, or a moderator has made changes to the story.
**Solution:** If you need changes after the window closes, suggest tag or title changes using the **suggest** link instead.
