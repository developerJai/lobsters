# Comments -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for posting, replying to, and voting on comments.

---

## Prerequisites

- A registered Lobsters account (logged in)
- The story must be less than 90 days old to accept new comments

---

## Overview

Comments are the primary way to discuss stories on Lobsters. Comments are displayed in threaded (nested) format, sorted by a confidence-based algorithm. You can post top-level comments, reply to existing comments, upvote or flag comments, and edit or delete your own comments within time limits.

---

## Task 1: Post a Top-Level Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page by clicking the story title or the **comments** link.
2. Scroll down to the comment form at the bottom of the page.
3. Type your comment in the text area. Markdown formatting is supported.
4. Optionally, select a hat from the **Put on hat** dropdown if you want to comment in an official capacity.
5. Click **Preview** to see how your comment will look.
6. Click **Post** to submit your comment.

### Expected Result

Your comment appears at the top of the comment thread. You receive an automatic upvote on your own comment.

---

## Task 2: Reply to an Existing Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page.
2. Click the **reply** link below the comment you want to respond to.
3. A reply form appears below that comment.
4. Type your reply in the text area.
5. Click **Preview** to check formatting.
6. Click **Post** to submit your reply.

### Expected Result

Your reply appears nested under the parent comment. If the thread is too deep (18 levels maximum), the reply link will not appear.

---

## Task 3: Upvote a Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page.
2. Click the **score number** (or upvote arrow) to the left of the comment you want to upvote.

### Expected Result

The score increases by one. Click the score again to remove your vote.

---

## Task 4: Flag a Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page.
2. Click the **flag** link below the comment you want to flag (you must have at least 50 karma and the comment must not be your own).
3. Select a reason from the dropdown:
   - **Off-topic**
   - **Me-too**
   - **Troll**
   - **Unkind**
   - **Spam**

### Expected Result

Your flag is recorded. Click **unflag** to remove it. Comments with many flags are automatically collapsed.

---

## Task 5: Edit a Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page containing your comment.
2. Click your comment's timestamp link, or find your comment in the thread.
3. Click the **edit** link below your comment (available within 6 hours).
4. Modify the text in the comment form that appears.
5. Click **Update** to save changes.

### Expected Result

Your comment is updated. An "edited" indicator appears on the comment.

---

## Task 6: Delete a Comment

### Steps

1. Navigate to the story page containing your comment.
2. Click the **delete** link below your comment (available within 14 days).

### Expected Result

Your comment is removed from public view and shows as "[Author deleted comment]".

---

## Variations

### Using a Hat on a Comment

If you have been granted a hat (verified flair), you can select it from the **Put on hat** dropdown when posting or replying. This shows your official affiliation next to the comment.

### Disowning a Comment

For comments older than 14 days, the **delete** link is replaced by a **disown** link. Disowning transfers authorship to the "inactive-user" account while preserving the comment text.

### Viewing Your Threads

Navigate to **Threads** in the site header to see all comment threads you have participated in, with your comments highlighted.

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Thread speed limit reached, next comment allowed in X minutes"

**Cause:** You recently posted in a heated thread (one with many flags). The system slows down replies in flagged threads to cool down arguments.
**Solution:** Wait the indicated number of minutes before posting again. Moderators are exempt from speed limits.

### Reply link is missing on a deeply nested comment

**Cause:** The comment is at the maximum nesting depth (18 levels).
**Solution:** Post a new top-level comment instead, or write a blog post and submit it as a new story.

### Cannot edit a comment

**Cause:** The 6-hour editing window has passed.
**Solution:** There is no way to edit after the window closes. Consider posting a follow-up reply with corrections.
