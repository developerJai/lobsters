# Inbox & Notifications -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for managing your inbox and notifications on Lobsters.

---

## Prerequisites

- A registered Lobsters account (logged in)

---

## Overview

Your inbox is a unified view of all notifications you receive on Lobsters. It collects comment replies, mentions, and private messages in one place. Unread notifications are indicated by a count next to your username in the site header. You can browse all notifications, filter by unread items, and manage your notification preferences through your settings.

---

## Task 1: Check Your Inbox

### Steps

1. Look at the site header next to your username. If you have unread notifications, a number appears indicating the count.
2. Click the notification count (or click **Messages** and then **Inbox**) to open your inbox.

### Expected Result

You see a chronological list of all your notifications, including comment replies, mentions, and private messages. Unread items are visually highlighted.

---

## Task 2: Read a Comment Reply Notification

### Steps

1. Open your inbox (see Task 1).
2. Find the comment reply notification in the list.
3. Click the notification to view the comment in context on its story page.

### Expected Result

You are taken to the story page with the comment highlighted. The notification is automatically marked as read and the unread count in the header decreases.

---

## Task 3: Read a Mention Notification

### Steps

1. Open your inbox (see Task 1).
2. Find the mention notification (shown when another user references your username with an @ symbol in a comment).
3. Click the notification to view the comment where you were mentioned.

### Expected Result

You are taken to the story page with the comment that mentions you. The notification is marked as read.

---

## Task 4: Read a Private Message Notification

### Steps

1. Open your inbox (see Task 1).
2. Find the private message notification in the list.
3. Click the notification to open the full message.

### Expected Result

You are taken to the message view where you can read and reply to the message. The notification is marked as read.

---

## Task 5: View Only Unread Notifications

### Steps

1. Open your inbox.
2. Click **Unread** in the navigation links at the top of the page.

### Expected Result

Only unread notifications are shown. This helps you quickly find items you have not yet addressed.

---

## Task 6: Configure Which Notifications You Receive

### Steps

1. Click your username in the site header and then click **Settings**.
2. Scroll to the notification settings sections:
   - Under **Comment Reply Notification Settings**, check or uncheck **Receive E-mail** and **Receive Pushover Alert**.
   - Under **Comment Mention Notification Settings**, check or uncheck **Show in Inbox**, **Receive E-mail**, and **Receive Pushover Alert**.
   - Under **Private Message Notification Settings**, check or uncheck **Receive E-mail** and **Receive Pushover Alert**.
3. Click **Save Account Settings**.

### Expected Result

Your notification preferences are updated. You will only receive notifications through the channels you selected.

---

## Variations

### Notifications from Flagged or Low-Quality Comments

Comments that have been flagged or scored below a certain threshold may not generate inbox notifications. This prevents low-quality or disruptive content from cluttering your inbox.

### Email Notifications

If you have enabled email notifications in your settings, you will receive an email for each comment reply, mention, or private message in addition to the in-app notification.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Unread count shows a number but inbox appears empty

**Cause:** The unread notification may be from a comment that was later deleted or from a story that was removed.
**Solution:** Click through any remaining items or visit the **Unread** filter. The count will reset once all items have been viewed.

### Not receiving email notifications

**Cause:** Email notifications may be disabled in your settings, or the emails may be going to your spam folder.
**Solution:** Go to **Settings** and verify that **Receive E-mail** is checked under the relevant notification sections. Check your email spam or junk folder. Ensure your email address in your profile is correct.

### Mention notifications are not appearing in your inbox

**Cause:** The **Show in Inbox** option for mentions may be unchecked in your settings. Also, mentions from comments that were flagged or scored below the quality threshold may be suppressed.
**Solution:** Go to **Settings** and check **Show in Inbox** under **Comment Mention Notification Settings**. If the comment was flagged by multiple users, the notification may have been filtered out.
