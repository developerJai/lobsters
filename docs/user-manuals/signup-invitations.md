# Signup & Invitations -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for signing up and inviting others to Lobsters.

---

## Prerequisites

- To sign up: a valid invitation link from an existing Lobsters user
- To invite others: an account older than 70 days, karma of -4 or higher, and invitation privileges not disabled

---

## Overview

Lobsters is an invitation-only community. Every new user must receive an invitation from an existing member. This creates a chain of accountability -- each inviter is responsible for the behavior of the people they invite. After joining, established users can send invitations to others.

---

## Task 1: Sign Up with an Invitation

### Steps

1. Open the invitation link you received via email.
2. On the signup form, you see who invited you in the **Invited By** field.
3. Enter your desired **Username** (letters, numbers, hyphens, and underscores; up to 25 characters).
4. Enter your **E-mail Address**.
5. Enter a **Password**.
6. Re-enter the password in the **Password (again)** field.
7. Write a brief introduction in the **About** field (optional but recommended; visible on your public profile).
8. Click **Signup**.

### Expected Result

Your account is created and you are automatically logged in. A welcome message appears. You are redirected to the home page.

---

## Task 2: Invite a New User

### Steps

1. Click **Settings** in the site header to go to your settings page.
2. Scroll down to the **Invite a New User** section.
3. Enter the person's email address in the **Email** field.
4. Optionally, write a personal note in the **Memo** field.
5. Click **Send Invitation**.

### Expected Result

An invitation email is sent to the provided address containing a unique signup link. A success message confirms "Successfully e-mailed invitation to [email]." The invitation link is valid until used.

---

## Task 3: Request an Invitation (When Enabled)

### Steps

1. Navigate to the login page.
2. Click **Request an invite** (this option is only visible when the site has invitation requests enabled).
3. Enter your **Name**.
4. Enter your **Email** address.
5. Enter a **URL** to verify your identity (such as a personal website, GitHub profile, or company page).
6. Click **Submit Request**.
7. Check your email for a confirmation message.
8. Click the confirmation link in the email.

### Expected Result

Your request becomes visible to established users who can then send you an invitation. You will receive an invitation email if a user decides to invite you.

---

## Variations

### Open Signups Mode

Some Lobsters instances may have open signups enabled (no invitation required). In this case, the login page shows a **Sign up here** link that takes you directly to the signup form.

### Post-Signup Invitation Prompt

On some instances, after signing up you may be prompted to invite someone else. This is optional and you can skip it by navigating to the home page.

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid or expired invitation"

**Cause:** The invitation code has already been used, or it was marked as used by the system when a logged-in user tried to open the link.
**Solution:** Ask the person who invited you to send a new invitation. Each invitation link can only be used once.

### "Your account cannot send invitations"

**Cause:** One of the following conditions is not met: your account must be older than 70 days, your karma must be -4 or higher, and your invitation privileges must not have been disabled by a moderator.
**Solution:** Wait until your account is old enough, participate positively to build karma, or contact a moderator if you believe your invitation privileges were incorrectly disabled.

### Invitation request not appearing to other users

**Cause:** You have not yet confirmed your email address by clicking the link in the confirmation email.
**Solution:** Check your email (including spam folders) for the confirmation email and click the link. Your request only becomes visible after confirmation.
