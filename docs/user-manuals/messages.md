# Messages -- User Manual

> Step-by-step instructions for sending, reading, and managing private messages on Lobsters.

---

## Prerequisites

- A registered Lobsters account (logged in)

---

## Overview

Private messages let you communicate directly with other Lobsters users. Messages are organized into folders: your inbox for received messages, a sent folder for messages you have sent, and an unread filter for messages you have not yet read. You can compose new messages, reply to existing ones, and delete messages you no longer need.

---

## Task 1: Send a New Message

### Steps

1. Click **Messages** in the site header to go to your messages page.
2. Fill in the **To** field with the recipient's username.
3. Enter a **Subject** for the message.
4. Write your message in the **Message** field.
5. Optionally, click the **Put on hat** dropdown to select a hat if you want to indicate a verified affiliation.
6. Click **Send Message**.

### Expected Result

A success message confirms your message has been sent. The recipient sees the message in their inbox and receives notifications based on their notification preferences (email and/or push notification).

---

## Task 2: Send a Message from a User's Profile

### Steps

1. Navigate to the profile page of the user you want to message by clicking their username anywhere on the site.
2. Click **Send a Message** in the navigation area at the top of their profile page.
3. The **To** field is pre-filled with the user's name. Enter a **Subject** and write your **Message**.
4. Click **Send Message**.

### Expected Result

Your message is sent to that user. You are redirected to your messages page with a confirmation.

---

## Task 3: Read a Message

### Steps

1. Click **Messages** in the site header.
2. Click the subject line of the message you want to read.

### Expected Result

The full message is displayed. The message is automatically marked as read.

---

## Task 4: Reply to a Message

### Steps

1. Open the message you want to reply to (see Task 3).
2. Write your reply in the **Message** field at the bottom of the message page.
3. Optionally, select a hat from the **Put on hat** dropdown.
4. Click **Reply**.

### Expected Result

Your reply is sent to the original sender. The conversation continues in both users' message threads.

---

## Task 5: Delete a Single Message

### Steps

1. Open the message you want to delete (see Task 3).
2. Click **Delete Message**.

### Expected Result

The message is removed from your view. The other participant's copy is not affected.

---

## Task 6: Delete Multiple Messages

### Steps

1. Click **Messages** in the site header.
2. Check the checkbox next to each message you want to delete.
3. Click **Delete Selected**.

### Expected Result

All selected messages are removed from your view. The other participants' copies are not affected.

---

## Task 7: Keep a Message as Unread

### Steps

1. Open the message you want to keep as unread (see Task 3).
2. Click **Keep As New**.

### Expected Result

The message is marked as unread again and will appear in your **Unread** filter as if you had not opened it.

---

## Task 8: Browse Message Folders

### Steps

1. Click **Messages** in the site header.
2. Use the navigation links at the top of the page to switch between views:
   - Click **Inbox** to see all received messages.
   - Click **Unread** to see only unread messages.
   - Click **Sent** to see messages you have sent.

### Expected Result

The message list updates to show messages matching the selected filter.

---

## Variations

### Wearing a Hat in Messages

If you have approved hats, you can select one from the **Put on hat** dropdown when composing or replying to a message. This shows your verified affiliation to the recipient.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Cannot find a message you received

**Cause:** You may have deleted the message, or it may be on a later page if you have many messages.
**Solution:** Check your **Inbox** view and use pagination to browse through older messages. Deleted messages cannot be recovered.

### Recipient did not receive your message

**Cause:** The recipient's account may be deactivated or banned, or they may not have email notifications enabled for private messages.
**Solution:** Verify that the username is correct and the account is active. The message will appear in their inbox the next time they log in, even if they did not receive an email notification.

### "User not found" when sending a message

**Cause:** The username you entered in the **To** field does not match any active account. The user may have changed their username or deactivated their account.
**Solution:** Double-check the spelling of the username. If the user recently changed their username, try their new username.
